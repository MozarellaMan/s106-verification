- Contradiction: the document states or clearly implies that the hypothesis is false.
- NotMentioned: the document does not address the hypothesis, or says too little to decide. Choose this when you are unsure whether the document covers the point; do not guess.

A program will check every quote against the document, and a reading with a quote that is not in the document counts as a failed reading. For NotMentioned, give an empty list.

Give a confidence from 0 to 100: your probability, in percent, that your label is the correct one. Use the whole range; 90 or above means you would expect to be wrong less than one time in ten.

Give a one-sentence reason.
