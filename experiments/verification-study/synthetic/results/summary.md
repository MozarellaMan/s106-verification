## Coverage by interval method, runs, and stated vs realised error

Monte Carlo error: a coverage figure from 200 replications has standard error sqrt(p(1-p)/200), which is 1.1 points at the nominal 97.5% and 2.1 points at 90%; the 95% Monte Carlo band is +/-1.96 se, so +/-2.2 points at 97.5% and +/-4.2 points at 90%. Each scenario's own se is in brackets below.

Two lower bounds for a1 from the same 1000 bootstrap draws: the percentile bound (the 2.5th percentile, what `estimates()` reports and the gate uses) and the basic (reverse percentile) bound 2 * a1_hat - q97.5. Only the percentile bound drives the gate, `estimated` and `confirmed`; the basic bound is a diagnostic. Limitation: `estimates()` does not expose its draws, so this crate repeats its resampling (`src/bootstrap.rs`: same cells, scheme list, seed and triplet formula) and checks in every replication that its 2.5th percentile equals the library's `lower` exactly; the basic bound is therefore only as current as that copy, and the check fails if `accuracy.rs` changes its resampling.

Runs: `confirmed` needs the gate to pass, so every wrong `confirmed` cell comes from a run in which the gate passed. The columns give in how many runs (of the replications) the gate passed and in how many at least one wrong cell reached `confirmed`.

Realised / stated: realised wrong count divided by the stated expected wrong count (the sum of `error()`), over (a) all all-agree-wrong cells, whether or not they reached `estimated` (every one is wrong, so this is 1 / their mean stated error), and (b) all `confirmed` cells, right or wrong (1 means the stated error is right on average; 5 means it is five times too small).

## Definitions

- true a1: the population accuracy of reader 1 (claude) on a random cell, including the effect of difficulty, shared corruption and stage; a2, a3 likewise for person and datahub.
- mean a1_hat (sd), bias a1: mean and standard deviation of the point estimate over replications, and mean minus true a1.
- coverage: share of replications whose (percentile) lower bound is at most the true accuracy (target 97.5% or more); Monte Carlo se and the basic-bootstrap coverage are in the second table.
- no est.: share of replications where no triplet formed; fits=false: share of the estimates the library calls a misfit (point estimate above 1 with a lower bound of 1 or more).
- gate pass: share of replications where reader 1 passes the gate (fits and lower >= 0.90); false-pass applies when true a1 < 0.90, power when true a1 >= 0.95.
- confirmed/rep: mean number of cells per replication that the ledger would mark `confirmed` (all readers agree, gate passed, error <= 1%, a non-LLM reader among them).
- confirmed wrong: observed share of confirmed cells whose agreed reading is actually wrong, with the count of wrong ones in brackets (target <= 1%).
- estimated wrong: observed share wrong among `estimated` cells (all agree, gate passed), with the number of such cells in brackets.
- all-agree-wrong cells: cells where every reader agrees on a wrong reading and `error()` gave a figure, with how many of them reached `estimated` and `confirmed`; their mean error is the posterior error `error()` gave them, with the minimum in brackets.

The error-bin reliability table (observed wrong share per posterior-error bin, estimated cells only) is in `cells.csv`.
