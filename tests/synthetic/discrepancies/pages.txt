DEED OF AGREEMENT pursuant to Section 106 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990
relating to land at Station Yard, Sample Street, Exampleton
=== page 2 ocr ===
"Development" means the construction of 100 new homes together with a community hall
"Market Housing" means the 70 dwellings to be sold on the open market
"Affordable Housing" means the 25 dwellings comprising Affordable Rent Units and First Homes
"Affordable Rent Units" means the 15 dwellings to be let at Affordable Rent
"First Homes" means those dwellings to be sold as First Homes
=== page 3 text-layer ===
The Development shall provide 100 new homes of which 25% shall be Affordable Housing
"Library Contribution" means the sum of £10,500 (ten thousand pounds)
"Sports Contribution" means the sum of £5o,000 (fifty thousand pounds)
"Healthcare Contribution" means the sum of £20,000 (twenty thousand pounds)
"Bus Stop Contribution" means a sum calculated in accordance with the formula in Schedule 2
=== page 4 text-layer ===
The Owner shall pay the Library Contribution prior to Commencement
The Owner shall pay the Sports Contribution prior to first Occupation
The Owner shall pay the Healthcare Contribution prior to Occupation of the 50th dwelling
The Owner shall pay the Bus Stop Contribution on demand
