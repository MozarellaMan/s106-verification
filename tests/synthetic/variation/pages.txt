DEED OF VARIATION
relating to an Agreement under Section 106 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990
land at Orchard Farm, Example Lane, Sampleford
=== page 2 text-layer ===
WHEREAS
(1) An agreement pursuant to Section 106 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 dated 4 March 2019 was made between the Council and the Owner (the Original Agreement)
(2) The parties have agreed to vary the Original Agreement as set out in this Deed
=== page 3 ocr ===
1. The definition of "Affordable Housing Units" in clause 1.1 shall be varied by deleting the number "12" and replacing it with the number "18"
2. Paragraph 3.2 of Schedule 2 shall be deleted
3. A new definition of "First Homes" shall be inserted into clause 1.1 as follows
"First Homes" means dwellings sold at a discount of not less than 30% of market value
4. The definition of "Open Space Contribution" in clause 1.1 shall be replaced with
"Open Space Contribution" means the sum of £30,000 (thirty thousand pounds)
=== page 4 text-layer ===
The Owner shall pay the Open Space Contribution prior to Occupation of the 10th Dwelling
