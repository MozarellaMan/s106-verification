# Claims this project will not make

What the project's outputs must not be read as saying. These limits follow from the project's
working rules and apply to every report, table and note built from this code.

- **No finding that an obligation was unmet.** A difference between what a deed agreed and what a
  dataset records is a discrepancy to check, never a breach. "Differs: review" means a person
  must read the borough's records.
- **No developer- or council-specific claim without its check level and evidence.** Since 14 Oct
  2026 no person reviews a claim before it names a developer or council; the automated checks
  stand in for that review, so every such claim carries the check level of the weakest cell it
  cites and the cells themselves.
- **Caution with causal claims.** Descriptive patterns are not causal evidence. That some
  approved developments were never built does not show that their obligations stopped them being built.
  Causal studies remain a later goal, if a defensible research design is possible.
- **No accuracy claim without human labels.** Until people have labelled obligations, ideally in
  held-out deeds, the pipeline has no measured accuracy. PASS means a value matches the model's
  own citation on the cited page, nothing more.
- **No mixing of measurement bases.** Percentages by units, habitable rooms or floorspace are kept
  apart and cannot be compared as if they were equivalent.
- **No original obligation reported as the current one.** A deed of variation can change what is
  owed, so the original figure is always labelled as original.
- **Unknown is not zero.** Unknown, unverified, inconsistent, varied and not yet due are different
  states and are reported as such. Failed retrievals and missing documents are recorded, not
  dropped.

Every claim the project does make traces to a document page, a dated dataset snapshot or another
identifiable source. Model confidence and agreement between models are not evidence.
